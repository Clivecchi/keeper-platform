Gloss-only. Not a build lock.

The “fourth surface” is mostly a naming collision. The locked member shell stays three panels: Nav · Center · Chronicle. The center panel is already a workspace — conversation by default, Library already overlays it. Stage belongs there as another state of the same Dialog, not as a fourth column.

Nav Config items currently replace Chronicle’s subject (keys/glossary outrank Dialog). That is why story disappears. The goal is “don’t lose the Document,” not “put Config over Dialog.”

Recommendation: keep EntityKind Config in Chronicle, but as overlay on a pinned Dialog/Document primary (the ChronicleView.primary/overlay split already exists for Acts). Library-like browsers stay over center. Do not move Key/Agent Config onto the conversation. Do not add a fourth panel.
