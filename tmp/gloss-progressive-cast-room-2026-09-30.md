Cursor · Progressive Cast Room (2026-09-30)

Gloss-only. Not a build lock.

Investigation name: Progressive Cast Room.

Diagnosis stands: Mechanism A gives every cued Agent the human prompt at once, then gives the finished replies to the Lead. That is independent consultation. It stays, as an explicit choice.

Proposed capability, separate from that: a shared Room.
Human speaks. Eligible Cast is the cued voice set, not the whole roster. Sure silence costs no model call. An offer call happens only when more than one cued voice is still ambiguous, on gpt-4o-mini, through a tiny prompt that does not enter the full agent stack. The Lead then directs whom to engage, or presents. One contribution runs, sees the Room delta from this turn, and changes the Room. The Lead looks at the changed Room and may direct once more, capped. Present uses the existing Lead message and castVoices.

Room events live on that message. Later turns rebuild a short digest the way the session action log rebuilds receipts. No second conversation system. No fixed Cloud-then-Rendr sequence. Tokens and latency fold onto the same message so a turn can say what intelligence it consumed. Estimated cost stays empty until a price table exists.

Do not implement from this Gloss.
