Every current and future Document renders through its Domain treatment. Becoming Together’s Document chrome is the look to keep: Forward present, Sections with weight, Points inside. That chrome belongs to the Document type, not to one Dialog.

Treatment paints it. Color, type, atmosphere come from the Domain. Finding the Plot in ke3p should feel like the same family as Becoming Together — same slots, Domain dress. A Document in another Domain wears that Domain.

Every Document can have a Forward. Forward is the directional objective of the Dialog — where the conversation is going, not the Document’s name and not a Becoming Together exclusive.

Do not copy Becoming Together’s four Path names (Progress, Known Issues, Development, Cast) onto other Documents. Those are that story’s Sections. The slot is universal. The names are authored.

Today: Chronicle already wraps Documents in ChronicleTreatmentShell. Forward already exists as Dialog fields. The miss is fill and honesty — new Documents get a manuscript and skip Forward, and Becoming Together’s Path colors are title-matched in the shell, not treatment tokens. That is why one Document looks designed and the next looks bare.
