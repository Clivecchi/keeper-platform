Cursor · Cast does not hear Cast (2026-09-30)

Gloss-only. Not a build lock.

Diagnosis: On a cued turn, Cast members generate in parallel. Each sees the human’s words, their own identity, the Dialog Document, and a session action log of receipts. They do not see the other members’ replies this turn, and ephemeral consults do not load the Dialog transcript as their own chat. Only Lead receives the real replies, in an orchestration block, and is told not to roll-call them because the voice cards already show the lines. That is why the room sounds like independent essays. Lead synthesis cannot become a conversation the Cast never had.

Forward options, not locked:
- Sequential hearing: later cued members receive earlier replies this turn, then Lead. Costs the latency the parallel path was built to avoid.
- Prior-turn hearing, still parallel: inject earlier Cast lines into each consult. Fixes continuity across turns, not this turn’s dialogue.
- Let Lead perform the dialogue: loosen the no-roll-call rule. Looks collaborative. Nobody heard anybody.

Recommendation if the aim is that they talk: sequential hearing for a small cued roster. Do not ask Lead to invent the conversation.
