Cursor · Stage presents a Composition (2026-10-03)

Gloss only. Not a build lock. No product code.

There is one Stage. Stage presents a Composition. A Composition is an arrangement for one pass. It is not always stored.

Context and the resolver produce truth. The Reading lists only claims and actions that truth supports. Rendr arranges those ids: order, grouping, emphasis, sequence, media, treatment pointer, and which existing Theatre sheet to use. Stage receives that arrangement and presents it in the one room. Rendr cannot add a claim, an action, or a subject the Reading does not already have.

KeeperStageComposition stays the stored story, presences, and stage theme. It does not grow Where-are-we fields. Realm Where are we? and Domain Where are we? are derived readings. The curated filmstrip is stored. A promoted Frame is live. A story pass may cite all three. Realm is its own context and does not present the anchor Domain’s stored room.

Continuation changes context and asks for a new Composition while the room stays Stage. Realm → Domain is that. Domain → Dialog is not decided here. stageArriving may still choose which resolver runs. It may not choose a second renderer.

Theatre.js sheets stay as they are: cover, slide, media, journey, moment, frame. Where-are-we cites do not require a new sheet.
