Does this make sense? Yes. Three nested truths, not one feature.

1. Findability is not ke3p-as-parent
- Register creates identity only. No Domain, no People row except pending email. That is why she vanished from the board even though she exists as a user.
- Invited people should never become orphans. Origin Domain is the relational authority (`invitedFromDomainId`). Fix the handoff; pending People should also say when that email now has an account (“registered, not yet arrived”).
- Do not auto-grant ke3p membership to invited people. That would hide the bug by putting her on the platform Domain instead of the life she was invited into.
- ke3p People as a platform arrivals directory is the right later surface for true uninvited self-serve registrants — Connection on ke3p so they are findable, not their life Domain. Domains stay peers.

2. People Dialog is the default first room
- Like a text / DM, but Keeper: Nav selects the person. Dialog is that private conversation. Chronicle is who they are.
- Lives on the origin Domain. Cast: inviter, invitee, origin lead. Not a Document to Keep or Present. Not a second invitation engine.
- This is where she sits down. Domain Dialogs (business, family, Generation Keeper) are doors from here, not the first screen.

3. Elevate the invite for the initiator
- Today: one briefing copied onto every Domain in the bundle. Person who / how they belong is shared. Why this Domain is not.
- Split seed: person (name, relation, about) once. Per object: why I am inviting, what I want them to understand, where I want them to participate.
- Chuck’s examples are the product: Bride — keep our life; Livecchi.biz — agency and public presence; Livecchi.us — family and Generation Keeper.
- Storage already has a row per Domain. The form is the gap. Later the object can be a Dialog, not only a Domain.

4. The origin lead walks the bundle. Each Domain lead keeps their own ask.
- On accept, ceox receives the person, this Domain’s ask, and introductions of the other invited Domains (name, their lead, their ask). Guide. Do not dump.
- The Livecchi.biz lead receives the person plus the business ask only.
- That is the same introduction pattern needed elsewhere: a short card for a Domain or an agent, not the other Domain’s memory. Isolation stays. Awareness is authored, not scraped.

Recommendation to lock
- Handoff fix first (previous Gloss). People pending shows “has account.”
- Invitation form: person seed + per-Domain ask. Same bundle.
- Arrival: People Dialog on origin Domain, lead in the room, doors to invited Domains.
- Introductions: Domain/agent cards injected when a bundle or later a conversation needs them.
- ke3p arrivals directory later, for uninvited registrants only.

Not this slice: ke3p as parent of Domains; a second DM product; dumping other Domains’ SOLE into the origin lead.
