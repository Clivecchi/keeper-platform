Cursor · Chronicle Trail is not the Cast projection (2026-09-30)

Gloss-only. Not a build lock. Withdraws the words “Room Digest.” Does not add a Room-specific structure.

Inspected Chronicle Trail in UniversalViewPanel.

Trail today is a client-side breadcrumb of Chronicle focus. Each entry is kind, id, and a label. Kinds are the Chronicle subjects: domain, dialog, journey, path, moment, keeper, draft, agent, and the rest of that list. It is appended when the focused subject changes. It lives in component state. It is not saved. Order is visit order. The bar shows the current crumb and two before it. There is no relevance judgment. The panel body renders the live subject, not the crumb. Clicking a crumb changes focus. Agents are not given this Trail.

A second, older footer log (trailStore) records navigation labels in memory, five deep, per domain. That is not Chronicle Trail.

The stack holds conceptually. Trace would record what happened. Trail would be the contextual projection of that record. Chronicle is already one surface: the right panel experiences a Trail as its breadcrumb, and experiences the current subject as presence.

Today’s Trail cannot feed Progressive Cast Room. It records where attention moved, not who spoke, what was offered, or which action ran. The projection Agents already receive is the session action log, built from action receipts. That is a receipt projection, not Trail.

Do not invent a Room Digest. Do not point Cast at the Chronicle breadcrumb and call it convergence. When factual Trace exists, the thing Agents encounter should be a Trail scoped to that turn. Chronicle remains a surface for a Trail. Same roles. The current breadcrumb is the focus scope only.
