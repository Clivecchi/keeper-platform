Cursor · Conversation Profiles sit above Vibe and Directed (2026-10-05)

Gloss only. Not a build lock.

The human-facing idea is three profiles: Conversation (talk with me), Cast (be in the room with me), Agency (work with me). Code does not have those three. The composer switch is only Current versus Conversation, and Conversation only shortens the Lead prompt. It does not decide who hears, and it does not decide whether Keeper may act. Dialog Style and Cueing are separate, and the profile is written so it must not change them.

Directed and Vibe are Dialog Styles, not profiles. Directed means the roster is visible and silent until the human cues someone. Vibe is the only style that auto-cues that roster and thereby opens the Progressive Cast room. Cueing: Directed is a third label: the Lead owns the turn. Ensemble, featured, and aside are reserved and unused.

On Be.Speak.Become the header can show Cast, Style: Directed, and Cueing: Directed at once, while the composer says Conversation. Those are four controls for overlapping ideas. The Already in Progress... turn was on the Conversation prompt, Directed style, empty cues, and plain_lead, and it still saved a Story because actions are not gated by the profile.

Proposal: the profile the human selects is what decides the room. Conversation does not routinely let the Cast hear, and does not routinely act. Cast uses the hearing Vibe already has: eligible voices may offer or stay silent, Kip directs, one relevant voice contributes. Agency uses that same hearing, and adds permission to act, with a receipt on the Trace. A manual cue remains an override, not the switch that turns the room on. Vibe stops being a competing style and becomes how Cast and Agency hear. Style and Cueing leave the header as user-facing modes.

Already in Progress... is an Agency ask: create and persist a Story. The intended path is hear, offer or silence, Kip directs, the selected agent contributes, Keeper acts, the receipt is visible, and the Story is there to open. Nothing goes to Stage until Take to Stage.
