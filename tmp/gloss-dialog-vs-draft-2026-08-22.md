Cursor · Dialog is the Document; Draft is a section (2026-08-22)

Gloss-only — not a build lock unless the Document already holds this.

We were missing the plot because Dialog and Draft were still being treated as one subject.

Selecting the Dialog must open the Document. Selecting the Draft must open that Draft and keep Talking in on its linked Dialog. A leftover draft URL must not put the Draft back after a Dialog click — that is why Chronicle showed Keeper UI Insights while Nav said Finding the plot, and why Points written to the Document could not be reached.

Drafts are sections of the Document (document_components plus dialog_id links). They are not a replacement for the Document, and they are not fiction-plot beats invented from a Dialog title.

Agents now get Talking in / Working on in the prompt. Points follow Working on. The Dialog title is a name. The conversation that actually happened is the source of Points.
