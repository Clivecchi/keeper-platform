Where the story lives today
Chronicle holds the kept story — the Document. Dialog view holds the conversation. Stage holds objects. A Turn from the lectern writes to the Dialog thread you cannot see, and may write Points you only see if you look right. That is why Stage feels like a surface of objects, and why the story feels ignored.

What Stage is not
Not a chat log. Do not put Dialog bubbles on the 70%. That would make Stage feel like Dialog.

What Stage must show
The story in development and in presentation. Objects stay. They are the cast and the props. The missing layer is the beat that is happening now — one current scene, not a transcript.

Recommended first presentation (before Rendr / Theatre)
