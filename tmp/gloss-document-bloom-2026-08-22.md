Cursor · Document bloom — Dialog writes the story (2026-08-22)

Gloss-only — not a build lock unless Chuck holds it on the Document.

Finding the Plot is a Dialog. A Dialog makes a Document. That Document is the conversational ground: Sections, Drafts, and Points live there. A Section may be one Point — an idea that does not yet fit. A Section may also hold several unstructured Points: weight, but less weight. A quieter room. Not the main stem.

Agents read the Document first. They do not forget the Domain. Broad context to a single focused Point. A story in a Domain may later be promoted out as its own Domain. While it is here, they stay keen.

Keeper keeps and tells stories. Written through conversation. Structured by agents who know they are driven by governance and by code. Where those two conflict, or lack the support the story needs in order to bloom — that lack is itself a Point.

The author is a co-creator. CRUD must be easy and full: Point, Section, Draft, Document.

Draft and Document should look like kin. Enough alike to feel one family. Different enough that opening the Document feels like entering the house, not another worktable.
