Cursor · Arrival is a Dialog at home (2026-09-27)

Built locally. Not a new lock. The 2026-09-26 arrival and the humans-in-the-room direction are now in the invitation path.

She accepts and lands on her Realm, in a Dialog. That Dialog names the origin lead as the guide, with Kip beside them, and lists doors into the invited Domains and the Dialogs assigned on the invite. Opening a door enters the Domain that owns it. The Dialog is not copied.

The inviter and the invitee are human members of the shared Dialog on the owning Domain, and of any Dialog assigned on the invite. They share one conversation there. People can also be added on a Dialog afterward, from that Domain’s members.

Extra Domains on one invite each have their own role. The default is Member. The phone keeps this Dialog in front. The owner’s first-visit welcome does not clear it.
