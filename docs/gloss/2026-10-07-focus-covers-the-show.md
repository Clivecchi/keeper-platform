Cursor · Focusing the box covers the show (2026-10-07)

Gloss-only. Not a build lock. Does not create Points or mutate the Document.

Chuck opened Stage. Something loaded. He clicked the message box to speak. The Dialog replaced what had loaded. He asked what the intended use is, because it sucks.

The code's intention: Stage stays the room. The box is how you talk without leaving. Focus on the box is Engage. Engage immediately yields. Any state but Present puts the Dialog in the center as a sheet, and the performance steps behind it. You are still On Stage in the nav. Click the dim background to return.

That intention is the failure. The show disappears before a word is sent. Nothing on the screen says the show stepped back so you can talk. The thing that loaded and the thing that replaced it feel like two products in one box.

Decision on the table (not locked): focusing the instrument does not cover the performance. A reply can arrive without taking the center. The show stays until someone asks to read the talk.
