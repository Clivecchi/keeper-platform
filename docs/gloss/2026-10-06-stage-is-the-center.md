Cursor · Stage is the center (2026-10-06)

Gloss-only. Not a build lock. Does not create Points or mutate the Document.

Chuck brought a presentation mock: curtains, a scene header, a center performance, a right-hand context, and a Composer that can change who you are acting as. The question was whether that environment is a new surface.

Code already has the environment. `V0Shell` loads the Domain and steps back. `UniversalBoard` is the persistent member place: top bar, left panel, center, right panel. `KeeperBoardPanelGroup` already calls the side panels curtains when the workspace is in stage mode, and can hide them when a presentation claims the room. "Universal" is the implementation name. It does not need to stay a visible word, and it should not be replaced by a new parent.

Stage, in the contract that should keep the name, is the center performance: `KeeperStageCanvas`, the pass from context and truth to a reading to a composition, and the stored `keeperStage` on the Domain. Rendr already arranges that center only. Dress is tokens. The reading is fixed.

The same word currently also means the room mode of the Board (`workspaceSurface: "stage"`). In that mode the transcript is replaced, the curtains lock, and Chronicle still sits on the right. The center column that holds orientation, performance, and the human instrument is `KeeperDialogFrame`. Composer is `AgentComposer` at the bottom of that frame, on Dialog and on Stage. Reach and Theme already invoke from Composer and present in Chronicle.

Where-am-I is split. The top bar playbill travels to another Domain (`SceneChange.travelToSlug` — a full shell load). The dialog banner names the Domain or Talking in / Working on. `StageOrientationBar` sits inside the canvas and names the pass. The right panel's Domain cover (`DomainFocusPresence`) is the focused object's body: identity, terrain, and config. It is not the scene header. Cast is cued from the composer and listed in the dialog header. The right panel is Chronicle.

Domain and agent are different records. House Frogmore, livecchi.biz, and veech.io are Domains. Kip and Ceox are agents. One chooser that mixes them is a later product decision. The existing Domain switcher is the one that changes the shell.

Decision on the table (not locked): do not add a top-level surface. Reserve Stage for the center performance. Treat the Board as the place that holds curtains, orientation, Stage, and Composer. First proof, if we build: lift orientation out of the canvas so those three are siblings; point one existing Composer action at Stage; open the existing Domain switcher from Composer. No Rendr design engine. No second identity system.
